# Feedback, Surveys and Business Goals

## Purpose
Re:Solve may collect structured client feedback and track lightweight business/operational goals without turning into an HR performance-management system.

## Feedback and surveys
Use cases:
- project completion feedback
- onboarding feedback
- service relationship feedback
- account review survey
- client NPS-style question where desired
- event/form-driven questionnaires

Chatwoot remains owner of support CSAT generated inside Chatwoot support workflows.

## Survey definition
A survey may use the Forms engine and define:
- purpose
- audience
- related organisation/project/service/property
- questions
- anonymous/identified policy
- distribution method
- open/close dates
- reminder policy
- reporting visibility

## Feedback record
Results should preserve source/context and privacy/consent policy. Free-text feedback may feed Client Success and Àríyá summaries only under caller permissions.

## Actions from feedback
Low/negative feedback can:
- create Attention
- create Request/task
- flag Client Health
- notify Account Owner
- trigger a relationship review

Do not automatically classify a client as at risk solely from one AI sentiment score.

## Business goals
Goals are lightweight operating targets, not employee appraisal.

Examples:
- sales revenue target
- qualified opportunity target
- renewal target
- receivables reduction target
- project completion target
- property-health target
- client onboarding target

A goal should declare:
- name
- owner/team/business context
- metric definition
- period
- target
- current measured value/source
- status
- notes

No per-employee performance-rating or HR review workflow is included.

## Forecasting
CRM/Sales may provide weighted pipeline forecasting based on opportunities, expected close and probability/stage assumptions. Forecast values must show assumptions and source period.

## Reports
Feedback and business goals may appear in Reports, Dashboard and Client Success where relevant.

## Àríyá
Àríyá may summarize survey themes or explain progress against goals using source evidence. It should distinguish AI interpretation from actual recorded scores/metrics.

## Acceptance criteria
- feedback reuses Forms/Notifications/Client Success
- Chatwoot CSAT remains separate
- goals are business/operational targets, not HR performance management
- forecast assumptions are visible
- AI sentiment is advisory rather than authoritative client-health truth
