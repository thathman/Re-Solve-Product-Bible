# Operational Communications and Announcements

## Purpose
Re:Solve needs a consistent record of important operational communications without becoming a second helpdesk or a full marketing automation suite.

## Communication activity
Permitted communications may be associated with Organisations/Contacts/Projects/Requests/Invoices/Properties when useful:
- email
- WhatsApp/Baileys
- SMS
- call log
- meeting note/reference
- client-visible update

External message truth remains with the relevant provider where appropriate; Re:Solve may retain provider-neutral metadata, delivery state and safe content/reference according to policy.

## Communication record
Potential metadata:
- channel
- sender identity
- sender/recipient references
- organisation/contact
- related record(s)
- direction
- subject/summary
- safe body/content where policy permits
- sent/received time
- delivery state
- provider reference
- attachments via Files where allowed
- consent/eligibility context
- activity/audit relationship

Do not store unnecessary copies of Chatwoot support messages; Chatwoot owns support conversations.

## Email delivery evidence
Where providers expose it, Re:Solve may record:
- queued
- accepted/sent
- delivered
- bounced/failed
- opened/clicked only where privacy policy and provider evidence support it

Open/click evidence must not be treated as absolute proof a human read the message.

## Announcements
Announcements are controlled broadcasts inside Re:Solve for staff or client audiences.

Examples:
- planned maintenance
- service notice
- portal announcement
- policy/process change
- holiday/support-hours notice
- important client-wide update

Announcement fields:
- title/body
- audience
- organisation/property/service scope
- start/end
- priority
- channels/surfaces
- acknowledgement requirement only where justified
- author
- status

Announcements are not marketing campaigns.

## Campaigns/marketing
Bulk marketing, newsletters and sophisticated campaigns belong to specialist connectors/plugins such as Brevo or future providers. Re:Solve may provide contact/segment context and record campaign references without rebuilding a marketing suite.

## Templates
Message templates use the Communications/Notification template systems and may be populated from safe declared variables.

## Privacy
Operational messages respect communication eligibility, data minimization and privacy settings. Marketing consent is separate from necessary service/billing/security communications.

## Àríyá
Àríyá may draft messages/announcements from authorized context. Sending remains a controlled action with recipient preview.

## Acceptance criteria
- important client communications can be linked to business context
- Chatwoot remains support-message truth
- provider delivery evidence is represented accurately
- announcements do not become a marketing suite
- bulk marketing remains connector/plugin territory
